FAQ

How long until my site is online?

Usually 7 days after you send photos, prices and your locations.

Do I have to deal with the technical side?

No. I handle hosting, updates and set-up. You send changes via WhatsApp.

Subscription or one-time – what is the difference?

Subscription: a setup fee, then monthly (Basic CHF 490 + CHF 190 per month, Premium CHF 790 + CHF 290 per month), cancel monthly, all changes included. One-time: CHF 1'990 (Basic) or CHF 2'990 (Premium), first year included, then CHF 390 per year for hosting, domain and small changes.

What does social media cost?

Social Start CHF 390 per month (8 posts and 4 stories), Social Plus CHF 690 per month, ads in your region CHF 350 per month plus ad budget paid directly to Meta. One-time setup CHF 290 (ads CHF 390), free for Offertenmeister website clients. Cancel monthly.

Can I keep my current address?

Yes. Your domain stays yours. Old pages are redirected to the new ones so nothing gets lost.

Is this also available for other trades?

Yes. The system also works for plasterers, electricians, plumbers and other trades.
